# Welcome to the Holi wiki  

**OBS this is still under development June 17 2025**

**Holi** (Holistic Ancient eDNA Pipeline) is an all-in-one bioinformatics framework specifically designed to process, analyze, and visualize ancient metagenomic data. It caters to the unique challenges of ancient eDNA (aeDNA), such as fragmentation, contamination, and damage, enabling better and more reliable taxonomic classification and authenticity assessment.  

Whether you're studying ancient metagenomes, paleoenvironments, or archaeological sediments, **Holi** can help you extract meaningful insights from complex datasets.

Explore this Wiki to learn more about Holi’s features, setup instructions, and workflow components.
